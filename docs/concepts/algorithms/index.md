# Algorithms

These pages describe calculations used by the in-process APIs. They focus on behavior that is visible through the public result models.


The indexing pages describe peak search, pixel-to-q conversion and orientation indexing. [Depth reconstruction](depth-reconstruction.md) describes wire-scan reconstruction, and [Monochromatic intensity versus Q](energy.md) describes reduction of a monochromatic image. Use these pages to interpret output and understand parameter effects. They are not a complete derivation of the 34-ID-E analysis methods. Physical coordinate labels, orientation conventions, the depth sign convention, and scientific tuning guidance require separate domain review.
